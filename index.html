How it works — Each letter pair (front[1]×side[1], front[2]×side[2]…) becomes one 3D module. Shorter words are padded.

Fonts — Upload any TTF/OTF/WOFF. Click "+ Font" to browse.

Editor — Expand the Pixel Editor to paint/erase silhouettes and join floating islands.

Export — Higher export resolution = finer voxels (same mesher as preview). Open STL in Cura / PrusaSlicer. Base and back export as boxes with the letters.

Merge bar — Stable English preview, watertight voxel letter mesh, STL loads in common slicers; layout and mesher covered by this checklist.
